# Final working directive — draft awaiting final evidence

## Objective and requirements

Deliver a small local operating loop for the selected bottleneck: disclosure engagement → qualified questions. Let synthetic invited people register, inspect a referenced fictional disclosure, save questions off-chain, receive a reasoned synthetic review and follow-up, and optionally explore a downstream acknowledgement without losing work.

Preserve canonical identity across duplicate aliases. Separate invitation from KYC, questions from qualified inquiries, review from wallet decisions, acknowledgement attempts from real transactions, and immutable experiment records from live sandbox actions. Never count a disclosure opening as proof of reading. Use current adjudication and matching eligible-person/cohort/time filters in numerator and denominator.

Use synthetic personas and local deterministic records only. No external sending, actual transactions, payment, deployment, production infrastructure or confidential materials. Follow [PRD](docs/prd.md), [journey/rules](docs/journey-and-rules.md) and [exactly five prioritized tasks](docs/backlog.md). Completion requires the mandatory wallet-refusal, duplicate-person and failed-acknowledgement demonstrations, valid measurement, channel experiment, operating decision and actual verification evidence.
